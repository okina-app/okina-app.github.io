Your body, read as a whole.

HOLOLISA reads your sleep, heart, training, food and lab reports, learns your own usual, and each morning tells you how ready you are. The work happens on your phone.

What it does

The screens show an example person's data.

  • Today

    Each morning, one answer: how ready you are, out of 100, against your own usual. Then what moved it, from your heart rate variability, resting pulse and sleep, and a short to do for the day.

  • The twin

    A 3D body that shows where today's readings live: sleep at the head, the heart at the chest, water and metabolism at the middle. Each place carries a plain word for how it is, and the one to watch stands out.

  • Train

    Today's session with its sets, weights and rest timer, and a word on whether your body is ready to push. A set is logged with one tap.

    Rest

    1:24

    Then set 3 of 4, 6 reps at 80 kg

  • Eat

    Log a meal by searching or by scanning the barcode on the packet. Protein, carbs, fat and water add up against your day as you go.

  • Body and labs

    Weight, body fat and the measurements you add, charted over time. Add a lab report from a photo: it is read on the phone, and each value is shown as your laboratory printed it, against that laboratory's own range.

    The photo is never stored or uploaded.

    Vitamin D

    74nmol/l

    Lab range 50125

    Rings are your earlier reports

  • Insights and HOLOLISA Age

    Your own normal for each signal, recovery over ninety days and the habits that went with it.

    HOLOLISA Age is a research estimate from nine markers on one blood test, using a published equation. It appears only when a report holds all nine, and HOLOLISA never guesses a missing one.

  • Patterns

    What tends to go with your better nights and mornings, found in your own data. For example, how your heart rate variability differs after the nights you were in bed by 22:45.

Your health data stays on your phone.

  • Worked out on the phone

    HOLOLISA reads Apple Health or Health Connect only with your permission, and does its work on the phone. No health reading, lab value or journal entry is sent anywhere in readable form.

  • Backup only if you turn it on

    Cloud backup is off until you say yes. Then your record is encrypted on the phone before it is uploaded, and kept in Frankfurt, in the EU, so a new phone can restore it.

    HOLOLISA's server keeps the key, so that signing in is enough to restore. The policy explains how.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No advertising, no analytics of our own, no tracking SDKs and no advertising ID. This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else.

    On Android, Google's ML Kit, the code behind the scanners, sends its own diagnostics. The policy lists them.

What HOLOLISA is for

HOLOLISA is for general wellness, for people 16 and over. It does not diagnose anything and does not replace a doctor. A band on a chart is your own usual, a lab value sits against the range your lab printed, and HOLOLISA Age is a research estimate. If you feel unwell, talk to a doctor.

HOLOLISA is in early testing.
