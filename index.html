The twin is you.

A 3D body built from your height, weight and body fat. It shows where today's readings live, and the one to watch stands out.

  • Your shape

    Change your weight or body fat and the figure follows. It builds from a male or female body, with no recognisable face.

  • Your state

    Sleep at the head, the heart at the chest, water and metabolism at the middle. Each place carries a plain word for how it is.

  • Tap a reading

    Each one opens its own screen, with the numbers behind the word.

  • One thing to put right

    When there is one, a plain line under the twin says it: “A glass of water would help.”

HOLOLISA Age

A research estimate from nine markers on one blood test, using the published PhenoAge equation. It appears only when a report holds all nine, and HOLOLISA never guesses a missing one. It is not a medical test.

Your health data stays on your phone.

HOLOLISA reads Apple Health or Health Connect only with your permission, and does its work on the phone.

  • Worked out on the phone

    No health reading, lab value or journal entry is sent anywhere in readable form. An account is optional.

  • Backed up only if you say yes

    Cloud backup is off until you turn it on. Then your record is encrypted on the phone before it is uploaded, and kept in Frankfurt, in the EU, so a new phone can restore it.

    Our server keeps the key, so that signing in is enough to restore. The policy explains how.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No advertising, no analytics of our own, no tracking SDKs and no advertising ID. This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else.

    On Android, the code behind the scanners sends its own diagnostics. The policy lists them.

Questions

Anything else, write to us from the support page.

What is HOLOLISA?

A wellness app for iPhone and Android. It reads what your phone and watch already measure, adds your training, food and lab reports, and shows how you are doing against your own usual, with one answer each morning.

Is it medical advice?

No. HOLOLISA is for general wellness. It does not diagnose anything and does not replace a doctor. A band on a chart is your own usual, a lab value sits against the range your lab printed, and HOLOLISA Age is a research estimate. If you feel unwell, talk to a doctor.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Cloud backup is off until you turn it on, and then your record is encrypted before it leaves the phone. The privacy policy lists everything that leaves the phone.

Which phones and watches work?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or an Android phone with Android 10 or later and Health Connect. A watch, ring or scale works when it writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. HOLOLISA has no watch app of its own.

What does it cost?

Nothing is sold in the app while HOLOLISA is in testing. The price for the release is not set yet.

Who is it for?

People 16 and over who want one clear answer each morning, and the detail behind it when they ask for it.

When can I get it?

HOLOLISA is in early testing. It is coming to iPhone and Android.
