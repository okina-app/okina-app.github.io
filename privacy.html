Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. OKINA is in early testing; this policy describes how the app works today and will be updated before public release.

Who we are

OKINA is developed by Kaiser Jonatan. Contact: kaiserjonatan911@gmail.com.

Your health data stays on your phone

OKINA reads health data (for example sleep, steps, heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, nutrition and workouts) from Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iPhone, only with your permission. It analyses that data on your phone. No health reading, lab value or journal entry is sent anywhere in readable form.

Exercise pictures are downloaded from a public image library; no personal data is sent with those requests.

What OKINA does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and no advertising ID.
  • Photos of lab reports and nutrition labels are read on the phone and are never stored or uploaded. Identifiers on them (name, date of birth, address, phone, e-mail, social security number) are removed before anything is read.
  • Your data is not sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Legal basis

Health data is processed with your explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), which you give at registration. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

Retention and deletion

The cloud keeps your latest backups. When you delete your account in the app, your account and backups are deleted immediately. Data on your phone is removed when you reset the app or uninstall it.

Processors

Supabase (hosting, authentication and storage, EU region); Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and you can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Hungary: NAIH). Write to the contact address above.
