Delete your HOLOLISA account

HOLOLISA is an app by the HOLOLISA team. Your health record lives on your phone; your account holds only what lets you sign in, an encrypted backup of that record, and a private comparison with one partner if you started one. Deleting the account removes all of it from HOLOLISA's server.

In the app

  • Android: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • iPhone: Settings → Account → Delete account.

The app also erases HOLOLISA's data on that phone, after the account is gone.

Without the app

Sign in with the email address of your HOLOLISA account. We send a code of 6 digits to it; with that code you can delete the account here.

No account is created here: an address without a HOLOLISA account gets no code.

Signed in as . Deleting the account permanently removes, from HOLOLISA's server:

  • the account and what lets you sign in;
  • your encrypted backups;
  • your profile photo and display name;
  • a private comparison you are in, with your scores, and your consent records.

It cannot be undone. The HOLOLISA app on your phone keeps its own data until you delete the app or erase it in Settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A security log entry that the account was deleted, no longer linked to you, kept for at most 400 days.
  • Backup files the server could not remove at once are removed by its nightly cleanup, within 24 hours.
  • The email with your code stays wherever your mail provider keeps it.
