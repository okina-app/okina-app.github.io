Support

Write to kaiserjonatan911@gmail.com. Tell us which phone you use and what happened. Please leave health readings out of the email; we don't need them to help.

Questions

Where is my data?
On your phone. HOLOLISA reads Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android, only with your permission, and works everything out on the phone. Cloud backup is off until you turn it on. The privacy policy lists everything that leaves the phone.
How do I move to a new phone?
On your old phone, turn on cloud backup. On the new phone, sign in with the same account, and HOLOLISA restores your record from the backup.
Can I keep a copy myself?
Yes. In the app, go to Settings → Data and privacy → Save to file.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Without the app, use the account deletion page. Either way the account, its backups and a comparison you are in are removed from HOLOLISA's server.
Is HOLOLISA medical advice?
No. HOLOLISA gives general wellness information about your own data. It does not diagnose anything and does not replace a doctor. If you feel unwell, talk to a doctor, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.
Who can use it?
People 16 and over.
When can I download it?
HOLOLISA is in early testing. It is coming to iPhone and Android.
